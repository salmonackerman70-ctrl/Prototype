ABOUT

QR-Based Campus Navigation System

This image shows the first part of our QR-Based Campus Navigation System project.

The system is designed to help students and visitors easily find important locations around the campus.

QR codes can be used to provide quick access to information about different areas of the school.

The campus map helps users understand the location of buildings, rooms, and other important areas.

This project aims to make campus navigation easier, faster, and more convenient for new students and visitors.

FLAGPOLE

School Flagpole

The flagpole is one of the important landmarks within the school campus. It serves as a recognizable reference point that can help students and visitors determine their location while navigating around the school.

It is located in a central area of the campus and can be used as a visual guide when finding nearby buildings and other important facilities.

Through the QR-Based Campus Navigation System, users can easily identify the flagpole and learn more about its location without having to manually search around the campus.

This landmark can also help new students become familiar with the layout of the school and understand where they are in relation to other destinations.

GULAYAN

School Gulayan

The Gulayan is an important area within the school campus where vegetables and plants can be grown and maintained.

It provides students with an opportunity to learn about gardening, plants, environmental care, and the importance of maintaining a healthy and sustainable school environment.

Through the QR-Based Campus Navigation System, users can easily locate the Gulayan on the campus map and identify its position within the school grounds.

The Gulayan can also serve as a useful landmark for students and visitors when navigating around the campus.

SCOUTS

Scouts

The North Building is located at the northern section of the campus, indicated by the yellow arrow on the map. It houses several classrooms and facilities used by students and faculty members.

Situated along the main thoroughfare leading toward the upper part of the campus, this building serves as a key reference point when navigating toward the northern grounds and adjacent facilities.

Through the QR-Based Campus Navigation System, users can quickly locate this building and view directions from their current position.

It is an important landmark especially for newcomers, helping them orient themselves as they move between the central area and the northern part of the campus.
